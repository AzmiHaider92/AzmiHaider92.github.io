PhD in Computer Science from the University of Haifa, advised by Dr. Dan Rosenbaum. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, generative modeling, and neural representations: learning compact representations of continuous signals, and building efficient methods for reconstruction and probabilistic inference from sparse or incomplete observations. My work spans tokenized neural fields and diffusion and flow-based generative models, with applications to 3D scene reconstruction, novel-view synthesis, physical fields, and image modeling.

I also hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Haifa and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the Technion. Alongside my studies, I worked at Intel on computer vision and deep learning, including 3D cameras, LiDAR, and face recognition.

Research Interests

  • Generative models for reconstruction
  • Representations of continuous signals
  • Fast amortized inference
  • Deep learning for computer vision
