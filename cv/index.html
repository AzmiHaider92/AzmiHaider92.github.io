Research Focus

Generative modeling, computer vision, and neural representations. My research develops methods for representation, reconstruction, and probabilistic inference across images, depth, 3D scenes, physical fields, and scientific data, using diffusion, flow matching, transformers, and tokenized neural fields, with an emphasis on uncertainty modeling and efficient inference.
